# FNP-Sales-Analysis-Excel-Dashboard

This project focuses on analyzing FNP (Ferns and Petals) sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify business trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and revenue patterns.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

# Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis

FNP (Ferns and Petals) that specializes in sending gifts for various occasions like Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries. The dataset contains details about the products, orders, customers, and relevant dates.

# Project Objectives

1. Total Revenue: Identify the overall revenue generated from all orders.

2. Average Order & Delivery Time: Calculate the average time taken to deliver orders.

3. Monthly Sales Performance: Analyze monthly revenue trends throughout 2023.

4. Top Products by Revenue: Identify the highest revenue-generating products.

5. Customer Spending Analysis: Calculate the average amount spent per customer.

6. Top 5 Products Performance: Compare the sales performance of the top five products.

7. Top 10 Cities by Orders: Identify the cities with the highest number of orders.

8. Order Quantity vs. Delivery Time: Analyze the relationship between order quantity and delivery time.

9. Revenue by Occasion: Compare revenue generated across different occasions.

10. Product Popularity by Occasion: Identify popular products for different occasions.

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Revenue: ₹35,20,984
* Total Orders: 1,000
* Average Customer Spending: ₹3,520.98
* Average Delivery Time: 5.53 days
* Order Quantity vs. Delivery Time Correlation: 0.00348

# Key Insights

* Generated total revenue of ₹35.20 lakh from 1,000 orders.
* Colors was the highest revenue-generating product category.
* February and August recorded strong sales performance.
* Imphal ranked first among the top 10 cities by order volume.
* The correlation value of 0.00348 indicated almost no linear relationship between order quantity and delivery time.

# Project Outcome

Developed an interactive Excel dashboard that provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, helps identify revenue opportunities, evaluates delivery efficiency, and supports better business decisions.
